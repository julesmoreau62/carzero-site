05Security9.60 km

Built to be trusted with a team's data.

Offline first

Everything opened is cached on the phone. Changes made without signal are queued and sent when it returns.

Your team, walled off

The database itself keeps each team's data apart. Rival teams on CarZero never see each other's plans.

Private files

Tickets and photos sit in private storage, served through short-lived links and per-person rules.

Your data stays yours

Two-step login for admins, a full history of changes, and an export of all your data at any time.

Hosted by Supabase, certified ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type 2, with daily backups. A security overview and a data processing agreement are available on request.
