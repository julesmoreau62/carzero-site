Who is responsible

For the data a team puts in CarZero (staff names, emails, phone numbers, schedules, rooms, tickets), the team is the data controller and CarZero acts as its processor. For this website and for accounts, CarZero is the controller: Tanguy Pavat, tanguypavat@carzeroapp.com.

What we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, role, phone number if provided.
  • Rally data entered by teams: schedules, cars, hotels, rooms, documents, tickets and photos.
  • Technical data needed to run the service (login sessions, notification subscriptions).

Why

To run the service: show each person their rally, send notifications about changes that concern them, and let team admins manage the rally.

AI import

If a team admin uses AI import, the content of the chosen file is sent to an AI service (Anthropic) to read it. Before sending, email addresses and phone numbers are removed.
